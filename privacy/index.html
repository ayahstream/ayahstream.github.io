Privacy policy

Effective November 23, 2026

Quran: Ayah Stream is an offline Quran reader. You can read, search and save passages without an account or internet connection. The Arabic text, English translation, navigation indexes and font come with the app. Reading a passage does not tell the publisher or the content providers which passage you opened.

What your iPhone saves

The app remembers your reading and study positions, liked ayat, bookmarks, saved passages and personal notes. Discover remembers recently opened passages, feed navigation and journey steps. If you set a reading goal, the app saves the goal and the days you mark complete. These records can reveal aspects of your religious practice.

The app also remembers onboarding, language, appearance, text size, reading layout and guide preferences. It stores this information in the app’s local settings. The app has no account-based sync, advertising, cross-app tracking or automatic analytics or crash-report upload service.

Optional usage counts

In Privacy & support, you can turn on “Save usage counts on this device.” It starts off. When enabled, it records reader opens, forward steps, context opens, onboarding completions, activated sessions, active-day counts, first and last active days, and whether you returned on day 1, 7 or 30. The summary excludes verse addresses, text, notes and account or device identifiers. You can delete the counts or turn the option off, which also clears them.

Sharing and external websites

“Share usage summary” opens the iOS share sheet with the counts described above. “Share report draft” opens it with your description, the selected passage reference if available, app version, edition and source address. A correction draft does not automatically include your notes or history. You choose whether to save or send the text and which app or recipient receives it. The destination handles any copy you share.

Source and license links open external destinations such as Tanzil, ClearQuran, GitHub, Open Font License and Creative Commons. A site you choose to visit receives a browser request that can include your IP address and browser information. The app adds no personal notes, reading history or tracking identifier to these links. The site’s privacy practices apply to your visit.

Removing data and managing backups

You can remove individual bookmarks and likes, erase a note, and clear recently opened passages in Discover. Clearing recently opened passages keeps separate feed navigation, saved passages and journey progress. To delete a note, clear its text in the note editor. When you edit or delete a note, the app removes earlier copies it can match to that ayah from the local edition-migration backup. The app also removes matched older copies for notes you already edited or deleted when it loads the bundled edition. The backup can still contain reading references and notes whose original address could not be matched to this edition.

Use Delete App on your iPhone to remove the app and its local data, including edition-migration copies. Offloading the app preserves its data. Your iCloud or computer backups may contain app data and may restore it later. Manage those backups separately in your device or computer backup settings. Deleting the app does not remove copies you have already shared with others.

Website and support

GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. See the GitHub privacy statement. This site loads its images and fonts locally or from your device and uses no analytics scripts.

You can email ayahstream.support@gmail.com about the app or to request deletion of a support message. Your email service and Gmail process your address, message and any attachments you send. The support mailbox receives them so we can answer and follow up. Correspondence may remain in the mailbox after a reply. Email the same address to ask about retention or request deletion. Please omit private notes and other sensitive details.
