FAQs

What do I need to use Quran: Ayah Stream?

Quran: Ayah Stream is designed for iPhone with iOS 18 or later. Once installed, you can read and browse the bundled Quran offline without an account. External links need an internet connection.

How do I change the reading language or layout?

Go to Home, then Settings. Under Read, choose Arabic, English, or Arabic + English and select a reading layout. You can also adjust the Arabic text size and app appearance in Settings.

How do I find a specific ayah?

In Read, tap the current reference at the top to jump by surah, juz, or page number. You can also search from Discover using a reference such as 2:255.

How do I keep my place or save an ayah?

Open Home and choose Continue reading to return to the saved position. Use the ayah actions to bookmark, like, or add a personal note. Find these items in Your library on Home. Your place and library are stored on your device.

How do I delete a personal note?

Open the note from Home → Your library, then clear its text. Changes save as you type. The app also removes older local migration copies it can match to that ayah. Delete App removes all app data stored on that iPhone. Offload App keeps it. Device backups and copies you shared are managed separately. See the privacy policy for details.

Where do I find the text and translation credits?

On Home, open Settings, then Sources & Licenses. The page lists the Arabic text, English translation, font, and navigation sources.

How do I report a possible text or translation issue?

Go to Home, then Settings, then Privacy & support. Choose Prepare a content issue report, describe what you noticed, then tap Share report draft to choose where to send it. Include the surah and ayah reference. The app sends the report only through a sharing action you choose.
